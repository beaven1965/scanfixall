## PART 1 — Messenger order flow (by hand, for GCash)

Use this when someone taps your ad and lands in the Messenger inbox of your "Beavs App" Page.
Reply yourself, or save each message as a **Saved Reply** in the Page inbox so you only tap and send.

**Message 1 — Welcome (send right away)**
> Hi! Thank you for your interest in ScanFixAll 😊
> It turns a messy photo of a paper into a clean document, and can shrink big photos too.
> You can try it free first here: https://scanfixall.netlify.app
> Are you looking to try it, or are you ready to get Premium?

**If "just trying" → Message 2A**
> Great! Open the link above on your phone, tap "Scan a Document", and take a photo of any paper.
> If you need help, just message me here. 🙂

**If "ready for Premium" → Message 2B (ask these 4 things)**
> Wonderful! Please send me:
> 1. Your name
> 2. Which plan: Family (₱250 / year) or Class (₱1,500 / year)
> 3. The email where I can send your access code
> 4. Are you ready to pay today? (Yes / Not yet)

**After they answer "Yes" → Message 3 (payment)**
> Thank you, [Name]!
> Plan: [Family / Class] — ₱[amount]
> You can pay by GCash:
> Number: [your GCash number]
> Name: [your GCash name]
> After paying, please send me a screenshot of the receipt. I will send your access code right after I see it.

**After the screenshot → Message 4 (send the code)**
> Payment received. Thank you! 🎉
> Your access code: [CODE]
> How to use it:
> 1. Open https://scanfixall.netlify.app
> 2. Tap Settings ⚙️
> 3. Type the code in the code box and tap Unlock
> Your code is good for 1 year.
> Questions? Message me here or email scanfixall@gmail.com

**If "not yet" → Message 5 (gentle follow-up, send next day)**
> Hi [Name], just checking in. Did you get to try ScanFixAll? 🙂
> If you have any questions before getting Premium, I'm happy to help.

### Tips
- Keep a simple list (Excel or your Beavs Apps Desk): name, plan, amount, date paid, code sent.
- Never send a code before you have seen the payment in your GCash.
- Reply within a few hours if you can. Fast replies get more buyers.

## PART 2 — UGC-style ad script (20–30 seconds)

UGC style = it looks like a real person talking to their phone, not a polished commercial. You, a friend, or a student can film it. Hold the phone upright, in good light, and talk naturally.

**Scene 1 (0–4 sec) — the hook. Hold up a crumpled paper.**
> "Ganito ba ang picture ng papel mo? Madilim, baluktot, hindi mabasa?"
> (English: "Does your photo of a paper look like this? Dark, crooked, hard to read?")

**Scene 2 (4–10 sec) — the problem**
> "Ako, ilang beses ko nang pinadala 'to sa office, tapos pinabalik. 'Pakiulit po, hindi malinaw.'"
> (English: "I sent this to the office many times and they kept sending it back. 'Please send again, it's not clear.'")

**Scene 3 (10–20 sec) — show the app on screen**
> "Tapos nakita ko 'to, ScanFixAll. Kukuha ka lang ng picture, pindot ng Clean, ayan, malinis na."
> (English: "Then I found ScanFixAll. Just take a photo, tap Clean, and it's clean.")
> Show the before and after on your phone screen.

**Scene 4 (20–27 sec) — the extras**
> "Pwede ka pang mag-shrink ng malalaking picture, at mag-sign sa screen. Hindi na kailangan mag-print."
> (English: "You can also shrink big photos and sign on the screen. No need to print.")

**Scene 5 (27–30 sec) — call to action**
> "Libre mong subukan. Pindutin ang link."
> (English: "Free to try. Tap the link.")
> On-screen text: **Try free — ScanFixAll**

### Ad text to put with the video
> Messy photo of a paper? Make it clean in under a minute. Shrink big photos. Sign on screen. Try ScanFixAll free. 👇

### Rules for your ads
- Say "Try free". Do **not** say "perfect" or promise results you can't guarantee.
- Do not show your own name or face if you want to stay anonymous. Film only the paper and the phone screen, and use a voiceover.
- Show a real before and after from the real app, not a mock-up.
- Send people to the Messenger chat first, or straight to the sales page: https://scanfixall.netlify.app/vsl/ (after it is published).
